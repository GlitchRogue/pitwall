Run your study session, then the cars pit for your break: the race goes red flag, the cars drive into the pit lane and your pit wall opens. Change tyres, engine mode and aero for the next stint, spend credits on R&D, or skip the break to restart early. Laps take about the real lap record time and cars are drawn to scale. Keys: Space start or pause, 1 to 7 camera, C swaps between your two cars.
